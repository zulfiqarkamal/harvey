# IT Infrastructure Services Agreement
**Contract reference:** KAP-IT-2018-003  
**Date:** 15 September 2018  
**Last amended:** 1 January 2021 (fee schedule only)  
**Parties:**
- **Customer:** Kapital Forsikring A/S, CVR 34567890, Farimagsgade 17, 1606 Copenhagen V
- **Supplier:** DataCenter DK ApS, CVR 76543210, Industriparken 55, 2750 Ballerup

---

## 1. Services

DataCenter DK ApS ("DCDK") shall provide co-location and managed mainframe services to Kapital Forsikring A/S ("Kapital") at DCDK's Ballerup facility.

Services include:
- Physical co-location of Kapital's mainframe hardware
- Power, cooling and physical security
- Network connectivity
- Remote hands services on request

## 2. Service Continuity

DCDK shall use best efforts to maintain continuous availability of co-location facilities. Planned maintenance shall be communicated at least 7 days in advance.

## 3. Physical Security

DCDK maintains physical security measures at its facilities including CCTV, access control and security personnel.

## 4. Confidentiality

Each party shall keep confidential all proprietary information received from the other party.

## 5. Liability

DCDK's liability for direct loss is limited to 6 months' service fees. DCDK shall not be liable for indirect or consequential loss.

## 6. Termination

Either party may terminate this Agreement with 12 months' written notice. Kapital may remove its hardware from DCDK's facility at any time upon reasonable notice.

## 7. Governing Law

This Agreement is governed by Danish law.
