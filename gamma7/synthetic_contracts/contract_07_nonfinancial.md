# Software as a Service Agreement
## ScandHR Analytics A/S and DataBridge Solutions Ltd.

**Contract Date:** 3 March 2024
**Reference:** SHR-SAAS-2024-042

---

### PARTIES

**Client:** ScandHR Analytics A/S, CVR 56789013, Østerbrogade 55, 2100 Copenhagen Ø, Denmark  
**Service Provider:** DataBridge Solutions Ltd., registered in England & Wales, 12 High Street, London EC1A 1BB, United Kingdom ("Provider")

Client provides HR analytics software to Nordic enterprises. Provider delivers a cloud-based data platform used to aggregate and analyse HR metrics.

---

## ARTICLE 1 — SERVICES

Provider shall make available to Client the following software services on a subscription basis:

- DataBridge Analytics Platform (web application)
- ETL pipeline service for HR data ingestion
- Scheduled report generation
- API access for Client's application integrations
- Standard technical support (business hours)

---

## ARTICLE 2 — PERSONAL DATA PROCESSING

### 2.1 Relationship

For the purposes of applicable data protection law, Client is the **data controller** and Provider is a **data processor** processing personal data on Client's behalf.

### 2.2 Categories of Data

Provider processes the following personal data:
- Employee identifiers and HR records
- Salary and compensation data
- Performance review data
- Diversity and inclusion metrics (special category data)

### 2.3 Processing Instructions

Provider shall process personal data only on documented instructions from Client. Provider shall immediately inform Client if Provider believes an instruction violates applicable law.

### 2.4 Subprocessors

Provider uses Amazon Web Services (Ireland) for infrastructure hosting. Additional subprocessors require Client consent with 30 days' notice.

### 2.5 Data Subject Rights

Provider shall assist Client in fulfilling data subject rights requests within 48 hours of Client's request.

### 2.6 Data Transfers

All personal data is processed within the European Economic Area. Provider shall not transfer personal data outside the EEA without Client's written consent and appropriate transfer safeguards.

---

## ARTICLE 3 — AVAILABILITY AND SUPPORT

### 3.1 Availability

Provider targets 99.5% monthly uptime for the Analytics Platform, excluding scheduled maintenance. Scheduled maintenance shall be conducted during low-usage windows (Sunday 00:00–06:00 CET) with 48 hours' notice.

### 3.2 Support Tiers

| Priority | Response Time | Resolution Target |
|---|---|---|
| Critical (platform down) | 2 hours | 8 hours |
| High (major feature broken) | 4 hours | Next business day |
| Normal | 1 business day | 5 business days |

---

## ARTICLE 4 — SECURITY

### 4.1 Technical Measures

Provider implements industry-standard security measures including:
- Encrypted data storage and transmission
- Access controls and authentication
- Regular security assessments
- Automated backup procedures

### 4.2 Security Incidents

Provider shall notify Client of any confirmed personal data breach within 48 hours of becoming aware.

---

## ARTICLE 5 — BACKUP AND RECOVERY

Provider maintains daily automated backups retained for 30 days. Full system restoration can be performed within 24 hours. Point-in-time recovery is available for the preceding 7 days.

---

## ARTICLE 6 — TERM AND TERMINATION

This Agreement runs for an initial term of 12 months from the Effective Date and renews automatically for successive 12-month terms unless either party gives 90 days' written notice of non-renewal.

Upon termination, Provider shall export Client's data in CSV format within 30 days. Data is deleted from Provider's systems within 90 days of export confirmation.

---

## ARTICLE 7 — FEES

Annual subscription fee: DKK 180,000. Invoiced quarterly in advance.

---

## ARTICLE 8 — LIABILITY

Provider's aggregate liability shall not exceed the fees paid by Client in the 12 months preceding the claim.

---

## ARTICLE 9 — GOVERNING LAW

This Agreement is governed by Danish law with the courts of Copenhagen having exclusive jurisdiction.
