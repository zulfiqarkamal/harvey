## 1. Services

ComplianceTools International BV ("CTI") grants Coastal Payment Institution A/S ("Coastal") a non-exclusive subscription to the ComplianceTrack GRC Platform ("Platform") as a cloud-based service.

The Platform provides:
- Policy management and document repository
- Risk register and control library
- Compliance monitoring dashboards
- Regulatory change tracking

## 2. Data and Infrastructure

**2.1** The Platform is hosted on Microsoft Azure infrastructure.

**2.2 Data residency:** CTI will use commercially reasonable efforts to store Customer data within the European Union. CTI reserves the right to process data outside the EU where required for Platform operations, support or disaster recovery purposes.

**2.3** CTI maintains ISO 27001 certification for its Platform operations.

## 3. Service Availability

CTI targets 99.9% monthly uptime for the Platform, excluding scheduled maintenance. Scheduled maintenance windows are published in the Platform status page. SLA credits of 10% monthly fee apply if availability falls below 99.5%.

## 4. Security

CTI shall implement technical and organisational measures to protect Customer data, including:
- Encryption in transit (TLS 1.2+) and at rest (AES-256)
- Role-based access controls
- Penetration testing at least annually
- SOC 2 Type II audit performed annually

## 5. Incidents

CTI shall notify Customer via email and in-Platform notification of any confirmed service outage within 4 hours of detection. For security incidents involving Customer data: CTI shall notify Customer within **72 hours** of confirming the incident.

## 6. Audit

Customer may request CTI's current ISO 27001 certificate and SOC 2 Type II report annually. Customer acknowledges that CTI operates a multi-tenant platform and that direct on-site customer audits are not operationally feasible.

## 7. Business Continuity

CTI maintains a Business Continuity Plan and Disaster Recovery Plan. Recovery Time Objective for the Platform is 8 hours. CTI reviews but does not test the BCP annually — testing would be disruptive to the production environment.

## 8. Data Processing

**8.1** CTI processes personal data as a data processor on behalf of Coastal as data controller.

**8.2** CTI shall process personal data only on documented instructions from Coastal, except as required by applicable law.

**8.3** CTI personnel with access to personal data are bound by confidentiality obligations.

**8.4** CTI shall implement measures pursuant to Article 32 GDPR.

**8.5 Sub-processors:** CTI uses Microsoft Azure (EEA regions), Datadog (monitoring), and Zendesk (support) as sub-processors. CTI shall notify Customer of material changes to sub-processors with 30 days' notice.

**8.6** Upon termination, Customer may export all data from the Platform within 30 days. Following this period CTI shall delete Customer data.

**8.7 Data subject rights:** CTI shall assist Customer in responding to data subject rights requests as technically feasible through Platform administrative tools.

**8.8 Breach notification:** CTI shall notify Customer of personal data breaches within 72 hours of confirmation.

## 9. Termination

Either party may terminate with 30 days' written notice. Customer may terminate immediately if CTI ceases to maintain ISO 27001 certification.

## 10. Governing Law
