# ICT Services Agreement
**Contract reference:** NBS-ICT-2023-0047  
**Date:** 15 March 2023  
**Parties:**
- **Customer:** Nordbank Sparekasse A/S, CVR 12345678, Kongens Nytorv 12, 1050 Copenhagen K
- **Supplier:** NordCloud Hosting A/S, CVR 87654321, Symbion, Fruebjergvej 3, 2100 Copenhagen Ø

---

## 1. Services

NordCloud Hosting A/S ("Supplier") shall provide cloud infrastructure services to Nordbank Sparekasse A/S ("Customer") as described in the Service Specification attached as **Exhibit A**.

Services include:
- Dedicated compute environments in the Supplier's Danish data centres
- Managed database hosting (PostgreSQL, Oracle)
- Automated backup services
- 24/7 monitoring and alerting

Sub-contracting: The Supplier may engage sub-contractors to fulfil its obligations under this Agreement. The Customer shall be notified of material sub-contracting arrangements within 30 days of their commencement.

## 2. Service Levels

The Supplier shall use commercially reasonable efforts to maintain availability of the Services.

**Uptime target:** 99.5% monthly availability, excluding planned maintenance windows.

Planned maintenance shall be communicated to the Customer at least 5 business days in advance. Emergency maintenance may be conducted without prior notice.

The parties shall review service levels annually.

## 3. Data and Security

**3.1 Data location:** The Customer's data shall be processed and stored in the Supplier's data centres located in Denmark (Fredericia and Ballerup facilities).

**3.2 Confidentiality:** The Supplier shall maintain strict confidentiality of all Customer data and shall ensure that personnel with access to Customer data are bound by appropriate confidentiality obligations.

**3.3 Security:** The Supplier shall maintain ISO 27001 certification for the duration of this Agreement and shall implement technical and organisational security measures appropriate to the risk.

**3.4 Data return:** Upon termination of this Agreement, the Supplier shall make Customer data available for export for a period of 60 days following the termination date, after which data will be securely deleted.

## 4. Incidents and Notifications

**4.1** The Supplier shall notify the Customer of any significant service disruption or security incident as soon as reasonably practicable.

**4.2** For incidents affecting the confidentiality or integrity of Customer data, notification shall include a description of the incident, the data affected, and the remediation steps taken.

## 5. Audit

**5.1** The Supplier shall maintain ISO 27001 and SOC 2 Type II certifications and shall make audit reports available to the Customer upon written request.

**5.2** The Customer may conduct on-site audits of the Supplier's facilities with 30 days' prior written notice, subject to reasonable confidentiality and security requirements.

## 6. Business Continuity

**6.1** The Supplier shall maintain a Business Continuity Plan (BCP) covering the Services.

**6.2** The Supplier shall perform an annual review of the BCP.

## 7. Termination

**7.1** Either party may terminate this Agreement for convenience with 90 days' written notice.

**7.2** Either party may terminate this Agreement immediately for material breach if the breach is not remedied within 30 days of written notice.

**7.3** The Supplier shall provide reasonable exit assistance for a period of 6 months following termination.

## 8. Regulatory Compliance

The Supplier acknowledges that the Customer is subject to financial sector regulation and agrees to cooperate with the Customer to meet applicable regulatory requirements, including providing information reasonably requested by the Customer's regulators.

## 9. Governing Law

This Agreement is governed by Danish law. Disputes shall be referred to the Copenhagen City Court as first instance.

## DATA PROCESSING AGREEMENT (Exhibit B)

**Pursuant to GDPR Article 28**

**Data controller:** Nordbank Sparekasse A/S  
**Data processor:** NordCloud Hosting A/S

### 1. Subject matter and duration
This DPA governs processing of personal data by NordCloud Hosting A/S on behalf of Nordbank Sparekasse A/S in connection with the ICT Services Agreement.

### 2. Nature, purpose and categories
- **Nature:** Hosting and storage of personal data
- **Purpose:** Provision of cloud infrastructure services
- **Data categories:** Customer names, account numbers, transaction data, employee data
- **Data subjects:** Bank customers and employees

### 3. Processor obligations
The Processor shall:
- Process personal data only on documented instructions from the Controller
- Ensure that authorised personnel are bound by confidentiality obligations
- Implement appropriate technical and organisational security measures
- Not engage sub-processors without prior written authorisation from the Controller, and notify the Controller of intended changes to sub-processors giving the Controller the right to object
- Make available all information necessary to demonstrate compliance with this Article
- Contribute to and allow audits conducted by the Controller

### 4. Security measures
The Processor shall implement measures including: encryption at rest and in transit, access controls, logging and monitoring, vulnerability management, and incident response procedures.

### 5. Data subject rights
The Processor shall assist the Controller in responding to data subject rights requests within the timeframes required by applicable law.

### 6. Breach notification
The Processor shall notify the Controller of any personal data breach **within 48 hours** of becoming aware of it.

### 7. Return and deletion
Upon termination, the Processor shall return or delete all personal data as directed by the Controller, with certified deletion confirmed in writing.

### 8. International transfers
No personal data shall be transferred outside Denmark or the European Economic Area without the prior written consent of the Controller.
