# ICT Services and Data Processing Agreement
**Contract reference:** NBI-ICT-2024-DOR-001  
**Date:** 20 June 2024 (drafted post-DORA)  
**Parties:**
- **Customer:** Nordisk Bank & Invest A/S, CVR 56789012, Bredgade 40, 1260 Copenhagen K
- **Supplier:** SecureVault Nordic A/S, CVR 65432109, Lautrupvang 2, 2750 Ballerup

---

## 1. Services and Sub-contracting

**1.1** SecureVault Nordic A/S ("SecureVault") shall provide document management and digital archiving services to Nordisk Bank & Invest A/S ("NBI") as described in Service Schedule 1.

Services include:
- Secure digital document storage and retrieval
- Document classification and retention management
- E-signature integration services
- Regulatory reporting document package preparation

**1.2 Sub-contracting:** SecureVault shall not sub-contract any element of the Services supporting NBI's critical or important functions without NBI's prior written approval. For other functions, SecureVault may engage pre-approved sub-contractors listed in Annex C (Sub-contractor Register). SecureVault shall notify NBI of any intended changes to sub-contractors at least 60 days in advance. SecureVault remains fully responsible for the performance of all sub-contractors.

## 2. Service Locations

**2.1** Services shall be delivered from SecureVault's data centres located in Denmark (Copenhagen and Aarhus). No processing shall occur outside Denmark without NBI's prior written consent.

**2.2** SecureVault shall notify NBI at least 60 days prior to any change in service locations.

## 3. Data and Security

**3.1 Availability:** SecureVault shall maintain 99.95% monthly availability for document retrieval services and 99.9% for ancillary services. Availability is measured as defined in Service Schedule 2 (SLA Metrics).

**3.2 Quantitative targets:** Recovery Time Objective: 2 hours. Recovery Point Objective: 1 hour. These are measured and reported monthly.

**3.3 Confidentiality:** SecureVault shall ensure that all personnel with access to NBI data sign individual confidentiality agreements. SecureVault shall provide NBI with a list of personnel with access upon request.

**3.4 Authenticity and integrity:** SecureVault shall maintain cryptographic audit logs for all document access events, ensuring authenticity and integrity of stored documents.

**3.5 Security standard:** SecureVault maintains ISO 27001:2022 certification (certificate number: SV-DK-ISO-2024-001, renewal due June 2027) and complies with the DORA ICT security requirements applicable to ICT third-party service providers.

## 4. Incidents

**4.1** SecureVault shall notify NBI within **24 hours** of detecting any service disruption or security incident affecting NBI's data or service availability.

**4.2** Notification shall include: incident description, affected systems and data, initial impact assessment, and remediation steps underway.

**4.3** SecureVault shall provide a final incident report within 7 business days.

**4.4** SecureVault shall cooperate fully with NBI in connection with any ICT incident report NBI is required to submit to Finanstilsynet or other competent authorities.

## 5. Audit Rights

**5.1** NBI shall have the right to audit SecureVault's compliance with this Agreement and applicable regulatory requirements, either directly or through a qualified third-party auditor appointed by NBI.

**5.2** NBI shall provide at least **45 days** prior written notice for planned audits, except in the case of incidents or regulatory requirements, where shorter notice shall be given.

**5.3** The Danish Financial Supervisory Authority (Finanstilsynet) and other competent authorities shall have the right to audit SecureVault in connection with their supervision of NBI.

**5.4** SecureVault shall make available all documentation necessary to demonstrate compliance with DORA Art. 30 and applicable data protection requirements.

## 6. Business Continuity

**6.1** SecureVault shall maintain a Business Continuity Plan (BCP) and Disaster Recovery Plan (DRP) covering all Services.

**6.2** SecureVault shall test the BCP and DRP at least **annually**, with live failover tests for critical systems. Test results and any identified remediation actions shall be provided to NBI within 30 days of each test.

**6.3** SecureVault shall cooperate with NBI's own business continuity exercises and drills.

## 7. TLPT Cooperation

SecureVault acknowledges that NBI may be subject to Threat-Led Penetration Testing (TLPT) requirements under DORA Art. 26. SecureVault shall cooperate fully with TLPT exercises where NBI's critical or important functions hosted by SecureVault are in scope, subject to reasonable operational safeguards.

## 8. Regulatory Cooperation

**8.1** SecureVault shall cooperate with Finanstilsynet, the Danish Resolution Authority, the European Banking Authority, and any other competent authority with jurisdiction over NBI or SecureVault in connection with their supervisory activities.

**8.2** SecureVault shall promptly notify NBI if SecureVault receives a regulatory inquiry or inspection request relating to the Services.

## 9. Termination

**9.1** Either party may terminate with 90 days' written notice.

**9.2** NBI may terminate immediately upon: (a) SecureVault's material breach not remedied within 15 days; (b) loss of ISO 27001 certification not restored within 30 days; (c) insolvency event affecting SecureVault.

**9.3** SecureVault shall provide exit assistance for a period of 12 months following termination, including data export, knowledge transfer and reasonable transition support.

**9.4** Upon final termination of exit period, SecureVault shall return all NBI data in a standard, machine-readable format (JSON/XML or as agreed) and provide certified confirmation of deletion of all copies.

## 10. Data Processing Agreement

**10.1 Roles:** NBI is the data controller. SecureVault is the data processor.

**10.2 Instructions:** SecureVault shall process personal data only on documented instructions from NBI, except as required by EU or Danish law, in which case SecureVault shall notify NBI unless prohibited.

**10.3 Confidentiality:** SecureVault personnel are bound by written confidentiality agreements. A register of authorised persons is maintained and available on request.

**10.4 Security:** SecureVault implements technical and organisational measures pursuant to GDPR Art. 32, including those described in Section 3 of this Agreement.

**10.5 Sub-processors:** SecureVault shall not engage sub-processors to process NBI personal data without NBI's prior specific or general written authorisation. Where general authorisation applies, SecureVault shall notify NBI of any intended sub-processor changes at least 60 days in advance, giving NBI the right to object. Any approved sub-processor is subject to the same GDPR obligations as SecureVault under Art. 28(4).

**10.6 Data subject rights:** SecureVault shall assist NBI within 5 business days in responding to data subject rights requests (access, erasure, rectification, restriction, portability).

**10.7 Breach notification:** SecureVault shall notify NBI of any personal data breach within **24 hours** of detection, enabling NBI to meet its 72-hour supervisory authority notification deadline.

**10.8 DPIAs:** SecureVault shall cooperate with NBI in conducting Data Protection Impact Assessments where processing is likely to result in high risk.

**10.9 Return and deletion:** Upon termination, SecureVault shall return or securely delete all personal data as directed by NBI, and provide written confirmation of deletion.

**10.10 International transfers:** No personal data shall be transferred outside the EEA without NBI's prior written consent and appropriate transfer mechanism (adequacy decision or SCCs).

**10.11 Audit:** SecureVault shall make available all information necessary to demonstrate compliance with GDPR Art. 28 and shall allow and contribute to audits under Clause 5.

## 11. Governing Law

This Agreement is governed by Danish law. The Copenhagen City Court has jurisdiction.
